Privacy Policy - Claw Wars

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version. Claw Wars has no accounts and no sign-up, and it plays offline. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or precise location. Your progress stays on your own device. The only data leaving your device is what our advertising provider, Google AdMob, collects to show ads.

This policy explains how Miav Games ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Claw Wars (package name com.clawwars.game, the "Game"), available on Google Play.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None that leaves your device. The Game stores your level progress, coins, upgrades, unlocked characters, daily task progress and settings in a file on your device. This file is not uploaded anywhere, is not backed up to the cloud, and is deleted when you uninstall the Game. We do not run servers, user accounts or analytics.

Opponents in the Game are computer-controlled. You do not play against or communicate with other people.

2. Information collected by third parties

The Game includes the software development kit (SDK) below. The provider acts as an independent controller of the data it collects. We only see aggregated reports.

3. What we never collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Precise or GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • Payment details - purchases are paid through Google Play; we never see your card or payment information
  • Account credentials - the Game has no login

4. Consent and legal basis

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game shows Google's consent form before personalised advertising is used. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Settings → Ad privacy options. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

Elsewhere, we rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game through advertising.

5. Children

Claw Wars is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us.

6. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Change your ad consent in the Game: Settings → Privacy Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Remove ads: an optional one-time purchase through Google Play removes banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain optional.
  • Uninstall the Game: deletes all locally stored data.

7. Data retention

We hold no personal data. Retention of data collected by Google is governed by Google's policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold no identifiable data, such requests are best directed to Google: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from partner apps. Deletion requests: data deletion page.

9. Data transfers

Google operates globally, so data may be processed outside your country, including the United States, under the safeguards in Google's policies.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
