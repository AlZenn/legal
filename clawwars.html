Privacy Policy - Claw Wars

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version. Claw Wars has no accounts and no sign-up, and it plays offline. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or precise location. Your progress stays on your own device. The only data leaving your device is what our advertising providers (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads) collect to show ads. You can change your personalized ads preference in the Game.

This policy explains how Miav Games ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Claw Wars (package name com.clawwars.game, the "Game"), available on Google Play.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None that leaves your device. The Game stores your level progress, coins, upgrades, unlocked characters, daily task progress and settings in a file on your device. This file is not uploaded anywhere, is not backed up to the cloud, and is deleted when you uninstall the Game. We do not run servers, user accounts or analytics.

Opponents in the Game are computer-controlled. You do not play against or communicate with other people.

3. What we never collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Precise or GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • Payment details - purchases are paid through Google Play; we never see your card or payment information
  • Account credentials - the Game has no login

4. Consent and legal basis

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Turkey, the Game asks on first launch whether personalized ads may be used, with Accept and Decline options, and advertising starts after you choose. If you decline, we ask again after about six months. Elsewhere, or if your country cannot be determined, no question is shown, personalized ads are on, and advertising starts when the Game opens. Your region is determined from your SIM, mobile network or phone region, so it may not always match where you are. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Settings → Personalized ads. When personalized ads are off, you still see ads, but they are not personalized; limited technical data, such as your IP address and device information, is still sent to the advertising providers so that ads can be shown.

Where we ask, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw in the setting above. Elsewhere, we rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game through advertising.

5. Children

Claw Wars is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us.

6. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Change personalized ads in the Game: Settings → Privacy Settings → Personalized ads.
  • Remove ads: an optional one-time purchase through Google Play removes banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain optional.
  • Uninstall the Game: deletes all locally stored data.

7. Data retention

We hold no personal data. Retention of data collected by Unity and Google is governed by their policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold no identifiable data, we cannot find or delete data held by the providers; such requests are best directed to them: Unity privacy policy, Google Privacy Policy. Deletion requests: data deletion page.

9. Data transfers

Unity, its advertising partners and Google operate globally, so data may be processed outside your country, including the United States, under the safeguards in their policies.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
