Privacy Policy - Arrowia

Last updated: 11 September 2026

The short version. Arrowia has no accounts and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location. Your game progress stays on your own device. The only data leaving your device is what our advertising, analytics and crash-reporting providers collect to show ads, count how the game is played, and tell us when it crashes.

This policy explains how Miav Games ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Arrowia (package name com.arrowia.game, the "Game"), available on Google Play.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None that leaves your device. The Game stores your level progress, star ratings, coin balance and settings in a file on your device. This file is not uploaded anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall the Game. We do not operate our own servers or user accounts.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game includes software development kits (SDKs) from the providers below. These providers act as independent controllers of the data they collect. We do not receive your personal data from them in an identifiable form - we only see aggregated statistics.

ProviderPurposeData collected
Unity Ads
(Unity Technologies)		 Showing advertisements, including rewarded ads you choose to watch Advertising identifier, app installation ID, IP address, device type and operating system, general location derived from IP (city / region / country), ad interaction and play session events
Google Analytics for Firebase Understanding how the Game is played so we can balance levels and improve it App instance identifier, device model and operating system, general location derived from IP, in-game events such as which level was started, completed or left
Firebase Crashlytics Detecting and fixing crashes Crash reports including device state at the time of the crash, operating system version and a random installation identifier
Firebase Remote Config Adjusting game settings without releasing a new version App instance identifier and basic device information used to deliver the correct configuration

3. What we never collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Precise or GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • Payment card numbers or bank details
  • Any account credentials - the Game has no login

4. Consent and legal basis

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised advertising is enabled. You can change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings → Manage ad consent. If you decline, you will still see advertisements, but they will not be personalised.

Where consent is not the legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interest in operating, funding and improving a free game.

5. Children

Arrowia is rated for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us and we will take steps to have it deleted.

6. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads. This resets the identifier used for advertising, or removes it entirely.
  • Opt out of personalised ads inside an ad. Unity ads include a Data Privacy icon you can tap.
  • Change your consent in the Game. Settings → Manage ad consent.
  • Remove ads. The Game offers an optional in-game option, unlocked with coins earned by playing, that turns off interstitial advertising. Rewarded ads remain available if you choose to watch them.
  • Uninstall the Game. This deletes all locally stored progress and settings.

7. Data retention

We do not hold personal data ourselves. Retention of the data described in section 2 is determined by each provider under its own policy. Analytics data in Firebase is retained according to the retention setting of the linked Google Analytics property.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold identifiable data about you, these requests are best directed to the provider concerned:

  • Unity Technologies - Privacy policy, contact DPO@unity3d.com
  • Google (Firebase and Google Play) - Privacy policy

You may also contact us and we will help where we can.

9. Data transfers

Our providers operate globally, which means data may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States. Those transfers are carried out under the safeguards described in each provider's privacy policy.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's release notes on Google Play.

Bu politika, Miav Games ("biz") olarak Google Play'de sunulan Arrowia (paket adı com.arrowia.game, "Oyun") mobil oyunuyla ilgili bilgileri nasıl ele aldığımızı açıklar.

1. Bizim topladığımız bilgiler

Cihazından çıkan hiçbir şey yok. Oyun; level ilerlemeni, yıldızlarını, coin bakiyeni ve ayarlarını cihazındaki bir dosyada saklar. Bu dosya hiçbir yere yüklenmez ve Oyunu kaldırdığında silinir. Kendi sunucumuz veya kullanıcı hesabı sistemimiz yoktur.

4. Onay ve hukuki dayanak

Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık veya İsviçre'deysen, kişiselleştirilmiş reklam etkinleştirilmeden önce Oyun senden onay ister. Tercihini istediğin zaman Oyun içinde Ayarlar → Reklam onayını yönet bölümünden değiştirebilirsin. Reddedersen reklamları yine görürsün, ancak kişiselleştirilmiş olmazlar.

Onayın hukuki dayanak olmadığı durumlarda, ücretsiz bir oyunu işletme, finanse etme ve geliştirmeye yönelik meşru menfaatimize dayanırız.

5. Çocuklar

Arrowia 13 yaş ve üzeri oyuncular için derecelendirilmiştir ve çocuklara yönelik değildir. 13 yaşından küçük çocuklardan bilerek kişisel bilgi toplamayız. Bir çocuğun böyle bilgi verdiğini düşünüyorsan bizimle iletişime geç, silinmesi için gereken adımları atarız.

6. Seçeneklerin

  • Reklam kimliğini sıfırla veya sil. Android'de: Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar. Bu, reklam için kullanılan kimliği sıfırlar veya tamamen kaldırır.
  • Reklamın içinden kişiselleştirmeyi kapat. Unity reklamlarında dokunabileceğin bir Veri Gizliliği simgesi bulunur.
  • Oyun içinde onayını değiştir. Ayarlar → Reklam onayını yönet.
  • Reklamları kaldır. Oyun, oynayarak kazanılan oyun içi coin ile açılan ve geçişli reklamları kapatan isteğe bağlı bir seçenek sunar. Ödüllü reklamlar, izlemeyi seçersen kullanılabilir kalır.
  • Oyunu kaldır. Bu, cihazda saklanan tüm ilerleme ve ayarları siler.
